# Teaching Notes & Preferences

- **Learner Profile**: Reader has ADHD (shorter working memory, high value on tangible wins, small friction threshold).
- **Format Requirements**:
  - Keep lessons tightly scoped (5-10 minutes to complete).
  - Tufte-style clean typography and visual clarity.
  - Interactive quizzes with immediate automated feedback.
  - Lead every response with the immediate next action.
  - Restate state every turn.
